About LOFT

Our story.

Born from founder Rosemary Larbi's passion for empowering young minds, LOFT connects families across the globe to rich African voices and traditions.

Our mission

Bedtime, turned into a moment of magic and meaning.

We nurture imagination, cultural pride and family connection through safe, ad-free storytelling. Every tale is crafted with love, so every child can drift off to sleep with a smile.
