How it works

Three steps to your first session.

  1. Book a visit

    Tell us about your school, your classes and what you would like the children to grow in. We plan the sessions with you.

  2. We run the sessions

    LOFT storytellers lead stories, games and activities with the heroes, in your classroom and on your timetable.

  3. Children keep growing

    Every child leaves with a story to keep practising: speaking up, trying again and being kind.
